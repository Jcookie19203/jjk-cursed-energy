# Dying, Wounded and Hero Points

## Dropping to 0 HP
When you hit 0 Hit Points you fall **unconscious** and gain **dying 1** (**dying 2** if the damage was a critical hit), plus your **wounded** value. Enemies usually stop attacking you, unless they're curses, which often try to devour the dying (see *Core Rules II §7*).

## Recovery checks
At the start of each of your turns while dying, roll a **flat check** with DC 10 + your dying value:
- **Critical success:** dying −2.
- **Success:** dying −1.
- **Failure:** dying +1.
- **Critical failure:** dying +2.

At **dying 4** you die (or at dying 4 − X if you're doomed X). If your dying value reaches 0, you're stable and unconscious at 0 HP. When you recover from dying, you gain **wounded 1**, or increase your wounded value by 1.

Taking damage while dying increases dying by 1 (by 2 on a critical hit).

## Waking up
You stay unconscious at 0 HP until you're healed or wake up naturally. **Wounded** goes away when you're healed to full or after 10 minutes of Treat Wounds.

## Healing that works here
Ordinary healing (Treat Wounds, healing items) can't restore **cursed-energy wounds**. **Reversed Cursed Technique** can, and so can a full night's rest. The **Training Wheels** dial *Cursed wounds can be medicined* turns this off.

## Hero Points
You start each session with **1 Hero Point** (2 with Training Wheels), and can hold up to 3. The GM awards more for great play.
- **Spend 1:** reroll a check and keep the new result.
- **Spend all (at least 1):** when you're about to die, avoid death. You stay at 0 HP, lose the dying condition, and become stable. You don't gain wounded from this.

The sorcerer sheet tracks Hero Points on its Main tab.
