# Checks and Degrees of Success

Almost everything uncertain is resolved with a **check**: roll a d20, add a modifier, and compare the total to a **Difficulty Class (DC)**.

## The four degrees
- **Critical Success:** your total is **10 or more above** the DC.
- **Success:** your total **meets or beats** the DC.
- **Failure:** your total is **below** the DC.
- **Critical Failure:** your total is **10 or more below** the DC.

A **natural 20** on the die improves the result by one degree. A **natural 1** worsens it by one degree. (The system rolls this for you and prints the degree in chat when it knows the DC or your target's AC.)

## What goes into a modifier
**Attribute modifier + proficiency bonus + other bonuses − penalties.**

- **Proficiency:** Untrained adds nothing. Trained, Expert, Master and Legendary add **your level + 2, +4, +6 or +8**. In this game, "your level" is the top of your Grade's PF2e band (Fledgling 2 … Special 20), and the Grade Table sets your ranks.
- **Bonuses and penalties come in types:** **circumstance**, **item** and **status**. Only the highest bonus of each type applies, and only the worst penalty of each type applies. Untyped penalties (like the multiple attack penalty) always add up.

## Types of checks
- **Attack rolls** target Armor Class (AC). In this game, Strikes and cursed strikes use your **Cursed Attack** bonus.
- **Saving throws** (Fortitude, Reflex, Will) are rolled to resist effects, against the attacker's DC (usually **Technique DC**).
- **Skill checks** and **Perception checks** use skills or Perception against a DC the GM sets or another creature's DC.
- **Flat checks** are a bare d20 with no modifiers (for example, the Black Flash check, or a DC 5 check to hit a concealed target).

## DCs you'll use
- A creature's **skill DC** or **Perception DC** is 10 + that modifier.
- Your **Technique DC** comes from the Grade Table.
- When the GM needs a DC for a task, a simple task is around DC 15 at low Grades; use the task's level for harder ones.

## Basic saves
Some effects say **basic save**. On a critical success take no damage, on a success half, on a failure full, and on a critical failure double. (Damage a card already calls "double" isn't doubled again.)
