# Turns, Actions and the Basic Actions

## Encounter rounds
Combat is played in **rounds** of about 6 seconds. Everyone rolls **initiative** (usually Perception) and acts in order. On your turn you get **3 actions** and **1 reaction**. Your reaction refreshes at the start of your turn.

- **Action icons:** ◆ one action, ◆◆ two, ◆◆◆ three, ◇ free action, ⟲ reaction.
- **Free actions** don't cost actions; **reactions** trigger off something specific and can happen on other creatures' turns.
- **Start of turn:** things like persistent damage, Steady Flow (+1 CE) and "until the start of your next turn" effects happen here.
- **End of turn:** you roll flat checks to end persistent damage and some conditions tick down (frightened drops by 1).

## The multiple attack penalty (MAP)
Each attack after the first on your turn takes a penalty: **−5** on the second and **−10** on the third and later. With an **agile** weapon, it's **−4** and **−8** instead. MAP resets at the start of your turn. It applies to every action with the *attack* trait, including cursed strikes and maneuvers like Grapple.

## Basic actions everyone can take
- **Strike** ◆ — attack with a weapon or unarmed attack against AC.
- **Stride** ◆ — move up to your Speed.
- **Step** ◆ — move 5 feet without triggering reactions that react to movement.
- **Crawl** ◆ — move 5 feet while prone.
- **Stand** ◆ — stand up from prone.
- **Drop Prone** ◆ — fall prone.
- **Interact** ◆ — use your hand: draw or stow an item, open a door, pick something up.
- **Release** ◇ — let go of something you're holding.
- **Seek** ◆ — Perception check to find hidden or undetected creatures or objects.
- **Sense Motive** ◆ — Perception check to tell if someone is lying or acting strangely.
- **Point Out** ◆ — show allies where an undetected creature is.
- **Take Cover** ◆ — behind cover or prone, gain greater cover (+4 AC and Reflex instead of +2).
- **Raise a Shield** ◆ — with a shield, gain its circumstance bonus to AC until your next turn.
- **Ready** ◆◆ — prepare a single action as a reaction with a trigger you choose.
- **Delay** ◇ — drop out of initiative and act later.
- **Sustain** ◆ — keep a sustained effect going until the end of your next turn.
- **Aid** ⟲ — after preparing on your turn, help an ally's check: +1 on a success, +2/+3/+4 at Master/Legendary, −1 on a critical failure.
- **Escape** ◆ — attack-trait check (unarmed attack, Acrobatics or Athletics) against the grabbing or restraining creature's DC.
- **Leap** ◆ — jump 10 feet horizontally (15 if your Speed is 30+) or 3 feet up.
- **Avoid Notice / Search / Scout** — exploration activities outside combat.
