# Skill Actions

Some skill actions need you to be at least **Trained** (marked *T*). DCs are usually the target's **save DC** (10 + that save) or **Perception DC**.

## Acrobatics (Dex)
- **Balance** ◆ — move across narrow or uneven ground.
- **Tumble Through** ◆ — move through an enemy's space against its Reflex DC; that space is difficult terrain.
- **Squeeze** (T), **Maneuver in Flight** (T).

## Athletics (Str)
- **Climb** ◆, **Swim** ◆, **Force Open** ◆, **High Jump / Long Jump** ◆◆.
- **Grapple** ◆ (attack) — against Fortitude DC. Success: grabbed. Critical success: restrained.
- **Shove** ◆ (attack) — against Fortitude DC. Success: push 5 feet (10 on a crit).
- **Trip** ◆ (attack) — against Reflex DC. Success: target falls prone (and takes 1d6 bludgeoning on a crit).
- **Disarm** ◆ (T, attack) — against Reflex DC. Success: −2 to its attacks with that item; crit: it drops the item.

## Deception (Cha)
- **Create a Diversion** ◆ — against Perception DCs, become hidden for a moment.
- **Feint** ◆ (T) — against Perception DC. Success: the target is off-guard to your next melee attack this turn.
- **Lie**, **Impersonate**.

## Diplomacy (Cha)
- **Gather Information**, **Make an Impression**, **Request**.

## Intimidation (Cha)
- **Demoralize** ◆ — within 30 ft, against Will DC. Success: frightened 1; critical success: frightened 2. A creature can't be Demoralized by you again for 10 minutes.
- **Coerce**.

## Medicine (Wis)
- **Administer First Aid** ◆◆ — stabilize a dying creature or stop bleeding.
- **Treat Wounds** (T) — 10 minutes, restores HP. *In this game it does **not** close cursed-energy wounds, unless your table uses the Training Wheels dial.*

## Occultism (Int)
- **Recall Knowledge** ◆ — identify curses, cursed techniques, cursed tools and barriers.
- **Identify Magic / Decipher Writing** — read talismans and seals.

## Stealth (Dex)
- **Hide** ◆ — while you have cover or concealment, become hidden against Perception DCs.
- **Sneak** ◆ — move at half Speed while staying hidden.
- **Conceal an Object**, **Avoid Notice** (exploration).

## Thievery (Dex)
- **Palm an Object**, **Steal**, **Disable a Device** (T), **Pick a Lock** (T).

## Other skills
**Arcana, Nature, Religion, Society, Crafting, Performance, Survival** and **Lore** all use **Recall Knowledge** for their subjects, plus their own exploration and downtime uses.
