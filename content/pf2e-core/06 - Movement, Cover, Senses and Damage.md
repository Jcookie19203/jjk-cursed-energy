# Movement, Cover, Senses and Damage

## Movement and space
- The grid is **5-foot squares**. Diagonals alternate 5 and 10 feet.
- **Difficult terrain** costs 5 extra feet per square; **greater difficult terrain** costs 10 extra.
- You can move through an ally's space, but not end there. You can't move through an enemy's space unless you **Tumble Through**.
- **Reach** is 5 feet for most Medium and Small creatures with most weapons, and 10 feet with reach weapons or cursed-energy extension.
- **Flanking:** you and an ally on opposite sides of an enemy, both able to act and in reach, make it **off-guard** to you both.

## Cover
- **Lesser cover:** +1 circumstance bonus to AC.
- **Standard cover:** +2 circumstance bonus to AC and Reflex saves, and Stealth to Hide.
- **Greater cover:** +4 to the same (Take Cover, arrow slits).

## Detection
- **Observed:** you see it normally.
- **Concealed:** fog, darkness or dazzled. Attacks need a DC 5 flat check.
- **Hidden:** you know which square it's in but can't see it. You're off-guard to it, and attacks need a DC 11 flat check.
- **Undetected:** you don't know where it is. Guess a square, and the attack needs a DC 11 flat check if you guess right.
- **Senses:** darkvision sees in darkness, low-light vision sees in dim light, and scent and tremorsense are imprecise senses.

## Damage
- Damage has a **type** (bludgeoning, piercing, slashing, fire, cold, electricity, acid, poison, sonic, mental, force, and so on).
- **Resistance X** reduces that damage by X. **Weakness X** increases it by X. **Immunity** ignores it.
- **Persistent damage** happens at the end of each of your turns. Then roll a **DC 15 flat check**; on a success it ends. Help from an ally can lower the DC to 10.
- **Critical hits** double damage.
- **Reinforcement** (*Core Rules I*): spend 1 CE when hit to reduce the damage by your Reinforcement value. The Apply button in chat offers this.

## Size
Tiny, Small, Medium, Large, Huge and Gargantuan. Large creatures take up 2×2 squares and usually have 10-foot reach.
