# Conditions

Conditions with a number (a **value**) get worse as the number rises. If you'd gain a condition you already have, keep the higher value. The character sheet tracks every condition below and applies the penalties it can automate; the token shows an icon.

## Valued conditions
- **Frightened X** — status penalty equal to X on all your checks and DCs. It drops by 1 at the end of each of your turns. *(automated)*
- **Sickened X** — status penalty equal to X on all your checks and DCs. You can't willingly eat or drink. Spend an action retching to attempt a Fortitude save to reduce it by 1 (by 2 on a crit). *(automated)*
- **Clumsy X** — status penalty equal to X on Dexterity-based checks and DCs, including AC and Reflex saves. *(automated)*
- **Enfeebled X** — status penalty equal to X on Strength-based rolls and DCs, including Strength-based damage and Athletics. *(automated for checks)*
- **Stupefied X** — status penalty equal to X on Intelligence-, Wisdom- and Charisma-based checks and DCs, including Will saves. When you use a concentrate ability, roll a flat check of DC 5 + X or it fails. *(checks automated)*
- **Drained X** — status penalty equal to X on Constitution-based checks such as Fortitude saves, and your maximum HP drops by your level × X. It drops by 1 after a full night's rest. *(automated)*
- **Slowed X** — you get X fewer actions at the start of your turn.
- **Stunned X** — you lose X actions (in total, across turns). "Stunned" with a duration instead means you can't act at all for that time.
- **Dying X** — see **Dying, Wounded and Hero Points**. At dying 4 you die.
- **Wounded X** — each time you start dying, add your wounded value to dying.
- **Doomed X** — you die at dying 4 − X.

## On/off conditions
- **Off-Guard** — −2 circumstance penalty to AC. *(automated)*
- **Prone** — you're off-guard and take a −2 circumstance penalty to attack rolls. Standing up costs an action. *(automated)*
- **Grabbed** — you're off-guard and immobilized. Manipulate actions need a DC 5 flat check. *(off-guard automated)*
- **Restrained** — you're off-guard and immobilized, and can't act except to Escape or force your way out. *(off-guard automated)*
- **Immobilized** — you can't use actions with the move trait.
- **Paralyzed** — you're off-guard and can't act except to Recall Knowledge or use purely mental actions. *(off-guard automated)*
- **Unconscious** — you can't act, you're off-guard, and you take −4 to AC, Perception and Reflex. *(off-guard automated)*
- **Blinded** — you can't see. Everything is hidden to you, and you're off-guard.
- **Dazzled** — every creature and object is concealed from you.
- **Deafened** — you automatically fail Perception checks that need hearing; sound-based effects may not affect you.
- **Concealed** — attacks against you need a DC 5 flat check to hit.
- **Fatigued** — −1 status penalty to AC and saves, and you can't do exploration activities. A Domain leaves you fatigued at 0 CE until you Reset.
- **Fleeing** — you must spend your actions moving away from the source.
- **Quickened** — you get 1 extra action at the start of your turn, usable only for what the effect says.

## Cursed-energy notes
- Damage with the *cursed* trait, and all Domain damage, causes **cursed-energy wounds**. Only Reversed Cursed Technique or rest restores them.
- Hard control from techniques (stunned, paralyzed, instant defeat) has the **incapacitation** trait. A target of a higher Grade than you treats its save as one degree better, and Domain sure-hits deal it half damage and reduce each condition by 1.
